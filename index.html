What this app stores

Nothing is uploaded, ever. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no outside files. It works offline once loaded.

When this is on, a simple list of words is kept in this browser only. Turning it off erases that list.

Reset erases everything this app saved in this browser, including the switches on this page.

About the sounds

The sound tiles are silent unless you add your own recordings (see “Sounds” below). The app does not use text-to-speech for letter sounds because it can't make true phonics sounds (it would say letter names or garble them). Until you add recordings, say each sound out loud together.

The app never grades pronunciation and never measures reading level. There are no scores, streaks or timers.

Sounds (your own recordings)

The tiles play only recordings you add. Nothing is generated, and nothing leaves this device. A lit tile plays its clip; after the blend, a whole-word clip plays if you added one.

  1. Record each sound in your own voice (Voice Memos works). Keep each clip about one second. Say the pure sound, like “sss” for s, not “suh”.
  2. Name each file by its tile, like s.m4a or sh.m4a. Whole words are named like word-sun.m4a. Use lowercase.
  3. Put the files in a folder named sounds next to index.html (on GitHub, upload the folder).
  4. Come back here and tap “Check sound files”.
